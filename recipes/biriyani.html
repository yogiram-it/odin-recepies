Biriyani

Biriyani is a mixed rice dish originating among the Muslims of the Indian subcontinent as a variety of Persian pilaf. It is made with Indian spices, rice, and usually some type of meat (chicken, beef, goat, lamb, prawn, fish) or in some cases without any meat (vegetarian), and sometimes, in certain varieties, eggs and/or potatoes.

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken or other meat
  • Indian spices
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the rice.
  2. Prepare the meat and spices.
  3. Cook the rice and meat separately.
  4. Layer the rice and meat in a pot.
  5. Cook on low heat until everything is well combined.
