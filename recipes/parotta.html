Parotta

Parotta is a popular South Indian dish made from wheat flour and filled with spiced meat or vegetables.

Home

Ingredients

  • Wheat flour
  • Spices
  • Meat or vegetables

Steps

  1. Prepare the dough using wheat flour and water.
  2. Roll out the dough into thin layers.
  3. Cook the layers on a hot griddle until golden brown.
  4. Fill with spiced meat or vegetables.
  5. Serve hot with curry or chutney.
